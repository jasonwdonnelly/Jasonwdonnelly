A standing ranking · updated continuously

Best Burgers in Denmark

Ranked by Jason Donnelly

Scored across 10 criteria, 0 to 10 each. Overall is the average. The list sorts itself.

32 spots ranked PRIME GRADE
How they’re ranked

Every burger is scored from 0 to 10 on ten things. The overall is the average of all ten, and the list sorts itself by that number. No single bad bun sinks a great patty, and no amount of sauce saves a sad one.

1
Patty flavor
Beefy, seasoned, juicy. Not a sad brown disk of obligation.
2
Patty texture
Good crust, tender middle. Smash-crisp or thick-sear, never a rubber puck.
3
Cheese melt
It should melt into the patty. Cold cheese slapped on top is a crime scene.
4
Bun quality
Soft but strong enough to survive. Toasted helps. A bun that disintegrates is edible packaging failure.
5
Burger ratio
Meat, cheese, bun, sauce, toppings all balanced. No lettuce sandwich wearing a beef hat.
6
Sauce / condiments
Moisture and flavor without turning it into soup. Bonus for a distinct house sauce.
7
Toppings
Fresh, crisp, purposeful. Pickles cut richness. Onions add bite. Tomato is not a wet apology.
8
Juiciness
Moist but not leaking like it has unresolved personal issues. Juicy, not structurally unstable.
9
Construction / eatability
Can you eat it without a shower, a fork, and legal representation?
10
Overall craveability
The real test: would you want another one later, or did you merely survive it?
